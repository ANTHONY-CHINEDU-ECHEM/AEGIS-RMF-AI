# Risk Management Plan

* Document ID: RMP_006
* Revision: C
* Device: Aurelia P200 Ambulatory Insulin Infusion Pump
* Status: Approved
* Related documents: SAS_001, HDS_002, SRS_003, UCS_004, VVR_005

This document describes a fictional device created for the Aegis RMF AI project. It is not derived from any commercial product.

## 1 Scope

This plan applies ISO 14971 cl 4.4 to the Aurelia P200 across design, production and post production. It covers the pump, the single use reservoir, the companion application interface and the labeling. The infusion set and the continuous glucose sensor are supplied by other manufacturers and are in scope only at their interfaces with the pump.

## 2 Responsibilities

The risk management lead owns the risk management file. Systems, hardware, software and human factors engineering each identify failure considerations within their specifications. Quality assurance confirms that every risk control measure is verified before it is credited. Clinical affairs owns benefit risk analysis. Post market surveillance supplies complaint and incident data.

## 3 Criteria for risk acceptability

Acceptability is decided with the five by five matrix of severity against probability defined in policy RAP_001. A risk in the ACCEPTABLE region needs no further action. A risk in the REVIEW region must be reduced further unless further reduction is not practicable, in which case a benefit risk analysis under ISO 14971 cl 7.4 is required. A risk in the UNACCEPTABLE region blocks design release. Any harm rated Catastrophic remains in the REVIEW region even at the lowest probability, so every risk of death is subject to benefit risk analysis.

## 4 Risk estimation method

Severity is taken from the harm catalogue and is a property of the harm. Probability before controls is the higher of the engineering estimate recorded in the design failure consideration and the rating implied by field data for similar incidents. Detectability is rated from the means of detection available before harm occurs. The risk priority number is the product of the severity, probability and detectability ratings and is used with the action priority lookup to rank work. Detectability and the risk priority number are prioritisation aids. They do not decide acceptability, which under ISO 14971 depends only on severity and probability of harm.

## 5 Risk control and verification

Risk control options are chosen in the priority order of ISO 14971 cl 7.1. A control is credited in the residual risk evaluation only when every requirement that implements it has a passing verification record. Information for safety may reduce a rating by one level at most across all such controls for a risk and may not reduce severity. Each control is reviewed for new hazards under ISO 14971 cl 7.5.

## 6 Overall residual risk and review

The overall residual risk is evaluated under ISO 14971 cl 8 once all individual risks have been evaluated. The risk management review under ISO 14971 cl 9 confirms that the plan has been carried out, that the overall residual risk is acceptable and that methods are in place to collect production and post production information.

## 7 Production and post production information

Complaints, service records and adverse event reports are collected under ISO 14971 cl 10.2 and reviewed every quarter under ISO 14971 cl 10.3. When the incident rate for a failure implies a higher probability rating than the engineering estimate, the higher rating is adopted and the affected risks are evaluated again under ISO 14971 cl 10.4.
